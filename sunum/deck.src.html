Umre Rehberim02
The challenge التحدي

Growing pilgrim numbers demand digital coordination at every step.

Vision 2030 aims to welcome 30 million Umrah pilgrims a year. Each journey depends on dozens of actors (agencies, guides, hotels, transport, merchants) who often still coordinate by phone and messaging groups.

Lost & separated pilgrims

Crowded sites, elderly first-time travelers and no live view of where a group member is.

Fragmented communication

Announcements spread through informal chats; guides cannot reach a whole group instantly.

Language barriers

Pilgrims arrive from dozens of countries; ritual and site guidance is rarely in their language.

Unregulated commerce

Little visibility into pilgrim spending, pricing fairness or merchant conduct around the holy sites.

Umre Rehberim15
Security & privacy الأمن وحماية البيانات

Built on least privilege and full traceability.

Identity & access

  • Managed authentication per user
  • Roles: pilgrim, guide/agency admin, general manager, super admin, merchant, staff
  • Server-side access rules on every collection; an agency only sees its own data

Privacy

  • Explicit consent screen before first use
  • National ID masked in the interface
  • Location sharing opt-in, visible to own agency only
  • Ready to align with the Saudi PDPL

Oversight

  • Audit log of sensitive actions (who, what, when)
  • Rule-based fraud alarms with review states
  • Merchant transactions tamper-evident and traceable
  • Secrets kept out of source control

Hardening roadmap before national rollout

Hosting in a Saudi cloud region or sovereign cloud · independent penetration test · server-side token service for the radio · NCA-aligned controls · encrypted backups and disaster recovery · 24/7 monitoring.
