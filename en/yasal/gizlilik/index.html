This Privacy Policy explains which personal data is processed, for what purposes, when you use the My Umrah Guide (Umre Rehberim) mobile app (“App”) and this website, and what your rights are. Your personal data is processed in accordance with Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”) and related legislation. For details, see the Personal Data Notice.

1. Data we collect

Data When Why
Name, email, profile photo When you sign in with Google (from your Google account) To create and identify your account
Phone, ID/passport details, room and hotel details When your agency registers you or you enter them Group organisation, your trip plan
Location (foreground and background) When you turn on location sharing Family Tracking, group live map, qibla and nearby places
Microphone (audio) When you press the talk button in the group walkie-talkie Real-time voice transmission
Camera, photos When you scan a QR code; when a merchant uploads a shop image Merchant discounts, shop listing
Step count When the pedometer feature is offered and you turn it on Daily step tracking
Notification token When you allow notifications Prayer time, announcement and group notifications
Purchase information When you buy a package Activating the package, invoicing
Device and crash logs When the App crashes Finding and fixing errors

Your card details are never stored in the App. In-app purchases are made through Google Play / the App Store; web payments are made on the PayTR secure payment page.

2. Permissions

The App asks for a permission only when you use the related feature; you can revoke it at any time in your phone settings. If you decline, only that feature stops working.

  • Location: Used in the foreground for qibla, prayer times and nearby places; for Family Tracking and the group map it is also used in the background once you turn sharing on. While sharing in the background, a persistent notification is shown; you can stop sharing with one tap in the App.
  • Microphone: Audio is captured and transmitted live only while you talk in the group walkie-talkie; conversations are not stored on the server.
  • Camera: Used to scan QR codes; camera images are not stored. Merchant accounts upload only the photo they choose as a shop image.
  • Physical activity (pedometer): When offered, step counts are calculated on your device and your explicit consent is requested separately.
  • Notifications: For prayer time reminders, agency announcements and group messages.

3. Who we share data with

  • Your agency: If you travel with an agency, your name, contact, room and (when sharing is on) location are shared with your agency’s authorised staff.
  • Family Tracking: Your location is visible only to people holding the link you created, while the link is valid.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase (authentication, database, notifications, crash reports), real-time audio infrastructure, Google Play / App Store and PayTR (payments), hosting.
  • Authorities: Only where required by law.

Your data is never sold or shared for advertising. The App contains no ads.

4. International transfers

Servers of Firebase and some service providers may be located outside Türkiye. Such transfers are made in accordance with Article 9 of KVKK. See the Personal Data Notice.

5. Retention

Your data is kept while your account is open. Location records are kept for the duration of your trip and package, then deleted. When you delete your account your data is deleted; payment and invoice records that must be kept by law are retained for the legally required period.

6. Account and data deletion

You can delete your account in the App via Profile > Delete my account. If you cannot access the App, follow the steps on the Account deletion page.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS); database access is restricted by authorisation rules. This website uses no cookies and no Google Analytics; visit statistics are cookie-free and do not identify you.

8. Children

Users under 18 should use the App under parental supervision. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Your rights

To exercise your rights under Article 11 of KVKK (access, rectification, erasure, objection, etc.), write to the data protection contact above.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page and significant changes are announced in the App.
