I have read and understood the Personal Data Notice. I give my explicit consent of my own free will to the processing below. I know that I can withdraw my consent at any time in the App settings or my phone’s permission settings, that withdrawal only stops the related feature, and that it does not affect earlier processing.

The App asks for each consent separately, only when you first use the related feature. Declining does not prevent you from using the other features of the App.

1. Location data

  • What: Your device’s real-time location; when sharing is on, also your location while the App is closed (background).
  • Purpose: Family Tracking link, your agency’s or group’s live map, reaching you if you get lost or separated.
  • Who sees it: Only people holding the Family Tracking link you created, members of your group and (on agency trips) your agency’s authorised staff.
  • Duration: While sharing is on; records are deleted when the trip and package period ends.
  • Withdrawal: One tap in Profile > Location Tracking, or in your phone settings.

2. Pedometer (data that may relate to health)

This feature is not offered yet; your consent will be requested separately when it is.

  • What: Step count from your phone’s motion sensor.
  • Purpose: Daily step tracking.
  • Where: Calculated on your device; only a summary (daily total) may be sent to the server.
  • Withdrawal: Turn off the pedometer or remove the “Physical activity” permission in your phone settings.

3. International transfer

  • What: Your account, contact, location and usage data.
  • To whom: Google Firebase, which provides the App’s infrastructure, and the real-time audio infrastructure provider (servers may be located abroad).
  • Purpose: Operating the service and storing data securely.

4. Contact

To withdraw your consent or get information, write to the data protection contact above.
