My Umrah Guide Features

A complete set of features designed to help you perform your worship correctly, safely and peacefully throughout your Umrah journey.

Worship Assistant

With you at every step, so you can perform your Umrah rites correctly and easily.

  • Step-by-step guidance for tawaf, sa’i, ihram and other rites
  • Prayers, dhikr and texts to recite
  • Frequently asked questions and practical tips on worship

Makkah and Madinah Guide

Discover the holy sites, historic places and key stops in Makkah and Madinah.

  • The Kaaba, the Prophet’s Mosque and important sites around them
  • Content rich in historical and spiritual information
  • Locations on the map and easy directions

Offline Audio Guide

Keep being guided by audio narration throughout your Umrah, even without an internet connection.

  • Offline audio guide content
  • Narration of worship steps and places
  • Battery-friendly, practical and uninterrupted use

Family Tracking and Group Walkie-Talkie

Stay connected with your family or tour group at all times and find each other easily, even in crowds.

  • See the location of family and group members
  • Quick, easy communication by walkie-talkie (instant voice)
  • Secure use, dedicated to tour groups
