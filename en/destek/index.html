What is My Umrah Guide and what does it do?

My Umrah Guide is an Umrah guide app that brings together prayers, the Holy Quran, prayer times and qibla, places to visit in Makkah and Madinah, the Haram gates and transport information. The basics are always free.

See all features

How do I sign up?

Open the app and sign in with your Google account using the “Continue with Google” button. If you travel with an agency, you can also sign in with the details your agency gave you.

If you want to delete your account, the steps are on the Account deletion page.

Account deletion

Which packages do you offer?

The basics are always free. Our paid packages:

  • Umrah Package: €6.29 (30 days · 1 person)
  • Family / Friends Package: €14.38 (30 days · up to 6 people)
  • Full Umrah Package: $1,500 (10 days · per person)

How can I apply as an agency or a merchant?

Fill in the application form on our pages for agencies and merchants. Once we receive your application, our team will contact you.

For agenciesFor merchants

How do payments and billing work?

All packages are purchased in the app. Payment is made through the PayTR secure payment system; your card details are not stored in the app. A VAT invoice is issued for your payment. For the Full Umrah Package, after your payment our manager approves your request and contacts you to set your travel dates.

For package and payment questions, include the payment date and the Google account you use in the app.

Compare packages

What should I do if I have a technical problem?

If location, notifications or the microphone do not work, check Settings > Apps > My Umrah Guide > Permissions on your phone.

If the problem continues, create a support request or call us, and briefly describe the problem and your phone model.
