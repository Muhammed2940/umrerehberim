About us

Who We Are

My Umrah Guide is a digital guide built to help you experience your Umrah journey more mindfully, peacefully and true to its meaning. It brings the information, planning and guidance you need together in one place, in a reliable and simple way.

My Umrah Guide is developed by Hekzapot Software.hekzapotsoftware.com

Explore the guide

Our Purpose

To make the Umrah journey easier by presenting what you need, from preparation to performing the rites, in a clear, reliable and practical way, and to contribute to a more meaningful experience of worship.

With You on Your Umrah Journey

My Umrah Guide accompanies you at every stage of your journey. Before you set out, it supports you with preparation, planning and the essentials; during Umrah, it helps you with the rites, the holy sites and practical information for a more mindful, peaceful and orderly experience.

Our Vision

To be a reliable, comprehensive and always accessible guide to the Umrah journey, helping more people experience this blessed journey mindfully and peacefully.

Our Values

  • Reliability

    We hold accuracy, care and responsibility at the heart of our content.

  • Spiritual Focus

    At every step we put the meaning and serenity of worship first.

  • Accessibility

    We offer clear, simple solutions that are easy for everyone to use.

  • Continuous Improvement

    We keep updating and improving our content with our users’ needs in mind.
