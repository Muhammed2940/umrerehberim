If your account was created by an agency (PIN sign-in), send your deletion request to your agency or to us as described below.

Deleted data

  • Your account and sign-in record
  • Your profile details (name, email, phone, photo)
  • Group Khatm groups you created; you leave the groups you joined
  • Location records you shared and your trip details

Records that may be kept

  • Records that must be kept under legal obligations
  • Payment and invoice records are kept for the period required by law and then deleted.

Your request is completed within 30 days at the latest.
