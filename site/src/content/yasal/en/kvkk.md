This notice is provided by the **data controller** named above under Article 10 of Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") and the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles for Fulfilling the Obligation to Inform.

## 1. Categories of personal data processed

- **Identity:** Name, surname; Turkish ID / passport number for agency registrations.
- **Contact:** Email, phone.
- **Visual:** Profile photo from your Google account; shop image for merchant accounts.
- **Location:** Real-time and background location when you turn on location sharing.
- **Audio:** Live audio while talking in the group walkie-talkie (not stored).
- **Data that may relate to health:** Step count, when the pedometer feature is offered.
- **Customer transactions:** Packages, purchases, support requests.
- **Transaction security:** Sign-in records, device information, crash logs.
- **Trip:** Hotel, room, stay dates, group information.

## 2. Purposes

- Managing membership and accounts, authentication
- Providing guidance services (prayer times, qibla, places to visit, audio guide)
- Group organisation, live map and announcements on agency trips
- Family Tracking and in-group walkie-talkie
- Package sales, payment and invoicing
- Responding to support requests
- Ensuring information security, fixing errors
- Fulfilling legal obligations

## 3. Legal grounds (KVKK Art. 5 and 6)

- **Performance of a contract** (Art. 5/2-c): account, packages and guidance services
- **Legal obligation** (Art. 5/2-ç): invoice and payment records
- **Legitimate interest** (Art. 5/2-f): security, crash logs
- **Explicit consent** (Art. 5/1 and Art. 6): location (especially background location and Family Tracking), pedometer data and international transfers. See the [Explicit Consent Statement](../acik-riza/)

## 4. Method of collection

Data is collected electronically through the App and website: via Google sign-in, registration by your agency, device permissions (location, microphone, camera, notifications) and information you enter.

## 5. Transfers

- **Within Türkiye:** Your agency (on agency trips), payment institution (PayTR), authorised public bodies.
- **Abroad:** Google Firebase (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.), the real-time audio infrastructure provider, Google Play and the Apple App Store. International transfers rely on your explicit consent or the other safeguards provided in Art. 9 of KVKK.

## 6. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

By applying to the data controller you may:

1. Learn whether your personal data is processed,
2. Request information if it has been processed,
3. Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly,
4. Know the third parties to whom it is transferred in Türkiye or abroad,
5. Request rectification if it is incomplete or inaccurate,
6. Request erasure or destruction under the conditions of KVKK Art. 7,
7. Request that rectification and erasure be notified to third parties to whom data was transferred,
8. Object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis,
9. Claim compensation if you suffer damage due to unlawful processing.

## 7. Applications

You may send your application, with information identifying you, to the **data protection contact** above by email or in writing to the company address, in accordance with the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller. Applications are concluded free of charge within 30 days at the latest.
